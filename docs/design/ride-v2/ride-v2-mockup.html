Ride screen v2: mockups

Back to the density of the first ride-panel mockup, with the newer style rules. Designed for the 400 px split view first; 800 and 1440 adapt from it. Mock data, Sweet spot 3×20′ at FTP 280.

  • Controls overlay: hover the coloured pacing box (or tap on a touch screen) for stop, previous, pause and next. B and D show it open; in the others, hover with the mouse to try it.
  • Always visible: mode and the − target + stepper share one row at the bottom (44 px), so slope changes stay one tap away.
  • Fields come from connected sensors: HR and cadence first, then SmO2, THb, core and skin temperature. Speed, distance, energy and climbed leave the ride view (they stay in the summary).
  • Flatter: the interval countdown sits on the page, not in a box. The pacing box is the only tinted surface. The interval avg and in-range values sit inside it instead of in extra tiles.
